### 11. DAILY UV STRATEGY

**Timing:**
- ✅ Get sun: Before 10am or after 4pm
- ❌ Avoid: 10am-4pm high UV index

**Protection:**

  - EltaMD UV Clear SPF 46 ($$)
  - Blue Lizard Sensitive SPF 30+ ($)

  - Coolibar, Solbari brands

**Check UV Index:**
- Use iPhone Weather app or UV Index apps
- SPF 30+ when UV index 3+
