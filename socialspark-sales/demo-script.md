# Sales Demo Script for Elliot & Ron
## SocialSpark AI for Tradies

---

## 🎯 OPENING (2 minutes)

**Setting:** Coffee shop, their office, or Zoom

**Your Opening:**
> "Thanks for meeting! I know you both hate doing social media, but you know it's important for getting calls. I've built something that handles it all for you.
>
> It's called SocialSpark - AI generates your posts, and you just click one button to post to Facebook. Takes 30 seconds instead of 30 minutes.
>
> Let me show you how it works for your business specifically."

**Transition:** Open laptop, go to dashboard

---

## 🖥️ DEMO (5 minutes)

### 1. Brand Setup (30 seconds)

**Show:** Brand settings panel

**Say:**
> "First, you set up your brand. Upload your logo, pick your brand color, enter your business name."

**Do:** 
- Upload a sample logo (or use placeholder)
- Pick orange color (tradies color)
- Enter "[Their Business Name]"
- Select "Tradies" industry

**Say:**
> "Done. Now the AI knows you're a tradie business and will generate content that matches your brand."

---

### 2. Generate Content (1 minute)

**Show:** Content generator

**Say:**
> "Now watch this. I click 'Generate New' and the AI creates posts specifically for tradies."

**Do:** Click "Generate New"

**Show:** 3 posts appear

**Say:**
> "Look at this first one - it's perfect for you:
>
> *[Read the emergency service post]*
>
> See how it has '🚨 EMERGENCY' and your phone number? This gets calls."

**Scroll to second post:**
> "This one offers free quotes - great for getting leads."

**Scroll to third post:**
> "And this one builds trust - shows you're licensed, insured, local."

---

### 3. One-Click Posting (2 minutes)

**Show:** Extension banner

**Say:**
> "Now here's the magic. You can install our browser extension - it's free with your subscription."

**Do:** Click "Install Extension" (show modal, then close)

**Say:**
> "Once it's installed, look at this post. I click 'Post to Facebook'..."

**Do:** Click "Post to Facebook" button

**Say:**
> "...and Facebook opens with the content already filled in. You just click 'Post' and you're done."

**Emphasize:**
> "No copy. No paste. No writing. Just click, review, post. Takes 5 seconds."

---

### 4. Facebook Groups (1 minute)

**Say:**
> "And you mentioned Facebook Groups - same thing. Click 'Post to Groups', go to your local community group, and post there."

**Say:**
> "Local groups are GOLD for tradies. 'Anybody know a good plumber?' - you're already there with helpful content."

---

### 5. Content Calendar (30 seconds)

**Show:** Calendar view

**Say:**
> "And this is your content calendar. See all the posts scheduled. Green dots are LinkedIn, blue are Facebook."

**Say:**
> "We post 3-5 times per week. Consistent presence without you thinking about it."

---

## 💰 PRICING CONVERSATION (3 minutes)

**Transition:** Close laptop or switch to pricing sheet

**Say:**
> "So here's the deal. I'm offering this to 3 tradies as beta customers. You get 60% off for the first 3 months."

**Show pricing:**

| Plan | Normal Price | Beta Price (You) |
|------|--------------|------------------|
| Starter | $49/month | Not available |
| **Growth** | **$497/month** | **$197/month** ⭐ |
| Agency | $997/month | Not available |

**Say:**
> "Growth plan is normally $497/month. For you, $197/month for 3 months. Then goes to full price."

**What's included:**
- ✅ AI content generation (unlimited)
- ✅ 3-5 posts per week
- ✅ Browser extension (one-click posting)
- ✅ Facebook and Facebook Groups
- ✅ Content calendar
- ✅ Analytics dashboard
- ✅ Month-to-month (cancel anytime)

**ROI Calculation:**
> "Think about it - one job from Facebook pays for 6 months of this. One emergency call, one renovation, one commercial job."

> "If this gets you just 2 extra calls per month, it pays for itself 10x over."

---

## ❓ HANDLE OBJECTIONS

### "I don't have time to learn this"
**Response:**
> "That's the point - you don't have to learn anything. The AI writes everything. You just click 'Approve' and 'Post to Facebook'. 30 seconds per week."

### "I tried Facebook before, didn't work"
**Response:**
> "Most tradies post randomly when they remember. This posts consistently 3-5x per week with professional content. Consistency is what works."

### "$197/month is expensive"
**Response:**
> "What's your average job worth? $500? $1000? $5000? This costs $6.50 per day. One customer pays for the whole year."

### "I need to think about it"
**Response:**
> "Totally understand. I'm only taking 3 beta customers and I have one other person interested. Want to secure your spot? No risk - month to month, cancel anytime."

**OR**

> "Fair enough. Can I ask - what's the main thing you're unsure about? Maybe I can clarify."

---

## ✅ CLOSE (2 minutes)

**If they're interested:**

**Say:**
> "Awesome! Here's what happens next:"

**Setup Process:**
1. **Today:** You pay first month ($197), I set up your account
2. **This week:** 30-min call to configure your brand/settings
3. **Next week:** First posts go live
4. **Ongoing:** You approve posts, click to Facebook

**Payment:**
> "I can send you an invoice or you can pay via bank transfer. What's easier?"

**Next Steps:**
> "Want to start today? I can have your first posts ready by Friday."

---

## 🎁 BONUS: Risk Reversal

**Say:**
> "Look, I want you to be happy. If after 30 days you're not getting value, I'll refund 100% no questions asked. Fair?"

**OR**

**First Week Free Trial:**
> "Tell you what - first week is free. I'll generate your content, you try it. If you like it, pay for month 2. If not, no hard feelings."

---

## 📋 IF THEY SAY YES

**Immediately:**
1. Get their business details (name, industry, phone, email)
2. Send invoice or get payment
3. Schedule onboarding call (30 min)
4. Add to your customer list
5. Celebrate! 🎉

**Follow up same day:**
- Send thank you message
- Confirm onboarding time
- Send login credentials

---

## 📋 IF THEY SAY NO

**Ask:**
> "No problem at all. Can I ask what would need to be different for this to work for you?"

**Get feedback:**
- Price too high?
- Don't see value?
- Not ready yet?
- Need different features?

**Stay in touch:**
> "I'll check back in a month. If you change your mind or want to try it, just give me a shout."

**Add to follow-up list:**
- Set reminder for 30 days
- Send occasional helpful content
- Keep relationship warm

---

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### Before the Meeting:
- [ ] Test the demo 3 times yourself
- [ ] Have backup laptop/phone ready
- [ ] Bring printed pricing sheet
- [ ] Prepare contract/simple agreement

### During the Meeting:
- [ ] Let THEM click the buttons (more engaging)
- [ ] Ask "What do you think?" after each feature
- [ ] Take notes on their reactions
- [ ] Watch their body language

### After the Meeting:
- [ ] Send thank you message within 2 hours
- [ ] If they said yes: Send invoice immediately
- [ ] If they said maybe: Follow up in 3 days
- [ ] If they said no: Follow up in 30 days
