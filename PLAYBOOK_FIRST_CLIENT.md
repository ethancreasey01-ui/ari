## 📧 PHASE 1: INITIAL OUTREACH

### The Trigger
Danny reached out via email thread with his web developer (Julie) and copywriter (Fiona). He mentioned:
- Cherry Builds was "completely dead" for leads
- He was "depressed" about marketing
- Had budget (paying Fiona $300/mo for blogs)
- Had unused assets ("heaps of photos")

### Key Insight
He was **already spending money** on marketing that wasn't working. This is a perfect client — pain + budget + untapped assets.

### What Made Him Convert
- **No upfront cost pitch** — "I'll do Phase 1 & 2 free, pay when you see results"
- **Speed** — "I can have ads running in 48 hours"
- **Education** — Showed him why $257/mo was too low (competitors spending $1,500-3,000)
- **Risk reversal** — Payment deferred until Phase 3

## 📝 PHASE 2: THE QUESTIONNAIRE

### Questions That Mattered

**1. Current Marketing Status**
- "What are you spending on marketing now?"
- "Have you run Google Ads before?"

**Why:** Established baseline and pain points.

**2. Budget Reality Check**
- "What monthly budget could you commit?"
- "What would 5-10 leads/month be worth to you?"

**Why:** Qualified him for $600-800/mo ad spend.

**3. Assets Audit**
- "Do you have before/after photos?"
- "Do you have a Google Business Profile?"

**Why:** Discovered he had untapped assets and critical gaps (no GBP).

**4. Business Context**
- "Average job value?"
- "Target suburbs?"
- "Why is Cherry dead while Aqua Tight works?"

**Why:** Understood his business model and seasonal factors (rain = waterproofing demand).

1. **No risk** — He pays nothing until he sees value

3. **Trust building** — Proves we can deliver before asking for money
4. **Anchoring** — $495 seems small vs competitors charging $1,500+/mo

## ⚠️ LESSONS LEARNED

### What Worked
✅ **Deferred payment** — removed all risk
✅ **Phased approach** — built trust progressively
✅ **Education** — showed him why his current spend was too low
✅ **Speed** — "48 hours" vs "next month"
✅ **Assets audit** — discovered untapped resources

### What Almost Didn't Work
⚠️ **Initial pricing** — $1,200 setup was too high, he balked
⚠️ **Mission Control complexity** — dashboard not essential for close
⚠️ **Technical jargon** — had to simplify "Google Business Profile" explanation

### What to Improve
🔧 **Get payment upfront** — even 50% would improve cash flow
🔧 **Contract** — simple agreement for scope/expectations
🔧 **Onboarding checklist** — systematic asset collection
🔧 **Testimonial ask** — build this in from day one
